How the numbers are made

VO₂max: anchored heart-rate regression

  1. Each run is resampled to a 2-second grid. Speed comes from distance, gradient from smoothed altitude.
  2. Oxygen cost of every sample uses the Daniels–Gilbert running curve, corrected for slope with Minetti’s gradient cost model.
  3. Only steady 60-second windows count: pace stable for 2 minutes, after a 5-minute warm-up, gradient under 8%, heart rate at 35–95% of reserve.
  4. A weighted, outlier-resistant line runs through those windows plus one physiological anchor, resting heart rate ↔ 3.5 ml/kg/min. Extending it to max heart rate gives the run’s VO₂max.
  5. Estimates are blended with a 14-day half-life by data quality, then fused with race-effort VDOT (hard efforts ≥85% HRmax) by inverse-variance weighting.
VO₂(HR) = a + b·HR fitted with anchor (HRrest, 3.5) VO₂max = a + b·HRmax

Endurance score

VO₂max sets the ceiling. How much of it you can hold for hours depends on the work behind it, so the ceiling is scaled by three saturating factors.

E = 170 · VO₂max · √(f(H)·g(L)) · h(D) f(H) = 1 − e^(−H/4) H = weekly hours (42-day) g(L) = 1 − e^(−L/75) L = longest run, min (6 wk) h(D) = 1.04 − 0.012·D D = HR drift % (≥ 60 min)

Tiers: Recreational <4000 · Intermediate 4000 · Trained 5500 · Well-trained 7000 · Expert 8500 · Superior 10000 · Elite 11500. The app’s own scale, set in a similar range to Garmin’s; not Garmin’s proprietary number.

Load, form and predictions

  • Training load is Banister TRIMP. Runs without heart rate get an estimate from grade-adjusted pace.
  • Fitness / fatigue are 42-day and 7-day exponential averages; form is the difference.
  • Heart-rate drift compares pace per heartbeat in the first and second half of a run.
  • Race times invert the Daniels VDOT formula, with a penalty on longer races when volume or long runs are short.
  • Every value is placed on the date and time recorded in the workout file, never the import date.

Getting files out of Garmin Connect

  • One run: open the activity → gear menu → Export Original (.zip with the .fit) or Export to TCX/GPX.
  • Everything: Account → Data Management → Export Your Data, then import the .zip files inside it.
  • Activity list: Activities → All Activities → Export CSV. Summary-level only, no map.

Files are processed in your browser. Only the parsed workouts are saved, to the storage chosen above.
