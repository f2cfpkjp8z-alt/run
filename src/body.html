How the numbers are made

VO₂max: heart rate against speed

  1. Each run is resampled to a 2-second grid. Speed comes from distance, gradient from smoothed altitude; uphill and downhill are converted to flat-equivalent speed with Minetti’s cost-of-gradient model.
  2. Only steady 60-second windows count: pace stable for 2 minutes, after a 5-minute warm-up, gradient under 8%, heart rate at 35–95% of reserve. Windows after 45 minutes count less (cardiac drift).
  3. Heart rate rises in step with oxygen use, and oxygen use rises in step with speed. So a weighted, outlier-resistant line runs through the windows and one anchor — resting heart rate at zero speed. Extending it to max heart rate gives your speed at VO₂max.
  4. VO₂max is the Daniels–Gilbert oxygen cost of that speed, the same VDOT scale as race results and Garmin-style predictions.
  5. Run estimates are blended with a 14-day half-life by data quality, then fused with race-like efforts (average HR near what an all-out effort of that length needs) by inverse-variance weighting; a race fades out over 60 days.
speed(HR) = b·(HR − HRrest) weighted fit, 2-pass Huber vVO₂max = speed(HRmax) VO₂max = −4.60 + 0.182258·v + 0.000104·v² (v in m/min)

Accuracy depends most on your max and resting heart rate. Without a measured max, the app uses the higher of your age estimate and your hardest run.

Endurance score

VO₂max sets the ceiling. How much of it you can hold for hours depends on the work behind it, so the ceiling is scaled by three saturating factors.

E = 170 · VO₂max · √(f(H)·g(L)) · h(D) f(H) = 1 − e^(−H/4) H = weekly hours (42-day) g(L) = 1 − e^(−L/75) L = longest run, min (6 wk) h(D) = 1.04 − 0.012·D D = HR drift % (≥ 60 min)

Tiers: Recreational <4000 · Intermediate 4000 · Trained 5500 · Well-trained 7000 · Expert 8500 · Superior 10000 · Elite 11500. The app’s own scale, set in a similar range to Garmin’s; not Garmin’s proprietary number.

Load, form and predictions

  • Training load is Banister TRIMP. Runs without heart rate get an estimate from grade-adjusted pace.
  • Fitness / fatigue are 42-day and 7-day exponential averages; form is the difference.
  • Heart-rate drift compares grade-adjusted pace per heartbeat in the first and second half of steady runs (interval sessions are skipped).
  • Race times invert the Daniels VDOT formula, with a penalty on longer races when volume or long runs are short.
  • Every value is placed on the date and time recorded in the workout file, never the import date.

Getting files out of Garmin Connect

  • One run: open the activity → gear menu → Export Original (.zip with the .fit) or Export to TCX/GPX.
  • Everything: Account → Data Management → Export Your Data, then import the .zip files inside it.
  • Activity list: Activities → All Activities → Export CSV. Summary-level only, no map.

Files are processed in your browser. Only the parsed workouts are saved, to this device or your online account.
